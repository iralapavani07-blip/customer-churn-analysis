Customer Churn Analysis – Key Insights

1. Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate.
Customers with flexible contracts are more likely to leave the service.

2. Customers with higher monthly charges show higher churn probability.

3. Long-term contracts (1 year or 2 years) reduce churn significantly.

4. Customers without technical support or security services tend to churn more.

5. The machine learning model predicts churn with about 81% accuracy.

Business Recommendations

• Encourage customers to switch to yearly contracts with discounts.
• Provide loyalty offers for high-charge customers.
• Promote support services to improve retention.
